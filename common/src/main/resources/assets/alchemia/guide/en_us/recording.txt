Recording

@unlock advancement alchemia:cauldron
@items right alchemia:alchemia_guide
So long as you have a guide on you, anytime you bottle a potion, it will record this into your guide. You can see these [[recipes|the Recipes chapter]].

To recreate a potion with a recipe you already have, you select the recipe in the book.
@page
Then with an empty hand and all tools and ingredients in your inventory, shift right-click a neutral cauldron, it will automatically brew the potion again using the exact steps when the potion was made.
