Creature Parts often have an inclination to seek out a specific effect, a good example of this is the ghast tear and regeneration.
@page
On their own they're quite useless, but if added to an unmixed but prepared cauldron, they'll shift the existing path towards regeneration.


There are many creature parts that lure to different effects, you'll need to experiment to find them, however.
