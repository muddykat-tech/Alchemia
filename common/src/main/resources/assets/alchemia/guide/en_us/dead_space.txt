While exploring the Potion Map, you can encounter red areas, these are zones that potions are instable in.
@page
@photo align=left angle=-1 size=square dead_space_2 dead_space_3
Movement within this area will turn the indication zone around the potion red.


If it turns fully red the potion is ruined, this can be recovered by normal movement outside the deadzone.
